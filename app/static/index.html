Neighbourhood Similarity Finder

Enter an address and pick a radius — we find named neighbourhoods in that area whose character most closely matches yours.

How does it work?

We count the amenities around your address — restaurants, cafés, nightlife, shopping, groceries, parks, waterfront, arts & culture, fitness, schools and public transit — within a ~1.5 km "neighbourhood" radius, using free OpenStreetMap data.

The same profile is built for a sample of neighbourhoods inside your search radius — up to 25, picked deterministically at evenly-spaced distances from your address, so both nearby and distant areas are represented. Each gets a % similarity score based on how closely its mix of amenities resembles yours — a similar mix in a much smaller or sparser area scores somewhat lower than one of comparable size. On each result card, grey bars are your neighbourhood's counts, blue bars are the match's.

The Feature importance sidebar lets you steer the results: every feature starts at Same — slide a category to More to prefer areas that have more of it than yours, or Less to prefer areas with less. Results re-rank instantly, no new search needed.

Tip: you can also click anywhere on the map to use that spot as your address. Clicking a result card draws the driving route on the map (self-hosted OSRM for BC/WA/FL; public demo server elsewhere), and cards show drive time when routing is available.

British Columbia, Washington State and Florida (one province, two states) are self-hosted on our own map database — searches there are fast and reliably return results. Other geographies work too; they just rely on the free public Overpass mirrors, which can take longer and are sometimes unavailable — trying again usually helps. Results are cached after the first search. Accuracy depends on OpenStreetMap data coverage, which varies by area.
