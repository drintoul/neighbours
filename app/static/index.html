Neighbourhood Similarity Finder

Enter an address and pick a radius — we find named neighbourhoods in that area whose character most closely matches yours.

How does it work?

We count the amenities around your address — restaurants, cafés, nightlife, shopping, groceries, parks, waterfront, arts & culture, fitness, schools and public transit — within a ~1.5 km "neighbourhood" radius, using free OpenStreetMap data.

The same profile is built for a sample of neighbourhoods inside your search radius, and each gets a % similarity score based on how closely its mix of amenities resembles yours — so a small town centre can still match a big city district if the proportions are alike. On each result card, grey bars are your neighbourhood's counts, blue bars are the match's.

The Feature importance sidebar lets you tune what matters: every feature starts at Same — slide a category to Less or More and results re-rank instantly, no new search needed.

Tip: you can also click anywhere on the map to use that spot as your address.

The first search can take a minute while we query open map servers; results are cached afterwards. Accuracy depends on OpenStreetMap data coverage, which varies by area.
